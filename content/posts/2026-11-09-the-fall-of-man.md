---
title: The Fall of Man
type: essay
summary: The modern man chases happiness everywhere except in reality. On rivers, fools, and the question of why we live at all.
---

The modern man is a contradiction. He destroys himself to prove that he loves himself.

He works like a slave, day and night, only to spend it all in seconds.

He lives to forget himself.

He never asks who he is, because if he did, he would have to accept reality. He would rather live in his fantasy than question it.

He chases happiness by every means possible except the one that works. He has forgotten that happiness is never reached through hedonism. It is reached only by accepting reality.

## Be like the river

You can only be happy if you walk through life the way water moves through a river.

Move forward, but never force it.

Be a source of life, as the river is to everything that grows along it.

Move through hardship the way the river cuts through mountain and rock: over time, not by force.

Don't waste your time on every inconvenience. A dirty river runs clear again, and your life will too.

The river does not judge the plants that grow beside it or question the animals that drink from it. So do not judge those around you. Learn who they are and understand what they need. Then help them, not by carrying them, but by teaching them to help themselves.

## The worst fool

Most of all, don't be like the modern man, who is full of himself and claims to know everything, yet never asks himself what he is.

The worst fool of all is the one who claims to know everything. If you already know it all, there is nothing left to learn, and that is where you become the true idiot. Not because you don't know, but because you refuse to know.

## The real question

In a world where God is dead, why do people want to live? What is the motive?

Some say happiness. But if happiness is the goal, does that justify killing for it? What about reaching it through drugs or crime? And even if you get that happiness, to what end? You don't keep it. Even a video game character has a bigger purpose than that.

Others say the purpose of life is survival of the fittest. But why should the fittest survive if there is no point in surviving at all?

If you keep nothing you gained in life, then there is no reason for your existence except chance: a random probability that happened to happen in the universe. You are a collection of atoms that landed in the right place at the right time, if you can even call it that. You are no different from a rock or a bug.

I refuse to believe that. Maybe that is just my bias. But there has to be something. There has to be someone out there. Maybe I am a program running on its computer. Maybe Earth is just a god's computer.
